Members

How it works

1. Tap New member card and type their name and phone.
2. They scan the QR that pops up. That opens their member card.
3. A friend scans the member's card and gets a 4-digit PIN for up to 2 free drinks right away.
4. They show the PIN with their meal. Type it in Free drink PIN above. It works one time. The member gets 1 point ($1) and sees who came in. A first online order counts too.
5. Paying a member out? Tap Redeem.
