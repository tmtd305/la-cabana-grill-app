How it works

1. Tap New member card and type their name and phone.
2. Send them their card link. They save it to their phone's home screen and show the QR.
3. People scan the QR, it opens our website, and when they place their first order the member gets 1 point ($1). Same person only counts once.
4. Walk-in they sent? Tap +1. Paying them out? Tap Redeem.
