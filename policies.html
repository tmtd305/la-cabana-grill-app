Policies

Ordering

Prices on the app include everything on the menu. Sales tax is added at checkout. Specials and deals are for a limited time and can change.

Pickup

Pick up at 6780 Collins Ave, Miami Beach. Most orders are ready in 15 to 25 minutes. Please show your order number at the counter.

Delivery

Delivery is handled by a third-party driver. The delivery fee is shown before you pay. Delivery times are estimates and can change with traffic.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Need to change or cancel? Call us right away at (786) 254-7968. Once your food is being cooked we may not be able to cancel. If something is wrong with your order, contact us and we will make it right.

Allergies

Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten, eggs, shellfish and fish. Tell us about allergies in the special instructions, but we cannot guarantee any dish is allergen free.

Your information

We use your name, phone and address only to prepare and deliver your order and, if you choose, to send you deals. We never sell your information. Payments are processed securely by Square.

Questions? Call (786) 254-7968.
